A familiar moment

The table moves faster than you do.

You understand a word here, a laugh there. By the time you build a sentence, the conversation has already moved on.

Maybe you work all day in English. Maybe your partner translates before you have the chance to try. Portuguese can be everywhere and still arrive one second too late when you want to speak.

  • You recognize more than you can answer.
  • You build the sentence in your head while the subject changes.
  • Everyday tasks take more energy because someone else has to help.
  • Apps have given you words, but not enough live practice.

You do not need perfect Portuguese to take part. You need a place where trying, being corrected and trying again can happen in time.

Exposure is not practice

Living near the language does not automatically make it yours.

Without deliberate speaking time, familiar words can stay slow. The cost is not a test score. It is another dinner, errand or conversation where your part gets shorter than you wanted.

How much of your life in Brazil is still being translated for you?

Recognise yourself in this? A short conversation is the fastest way to know where to start.

FAQ

Yes. The offer covers levels from zero to advanced.

Do I receive material?

Yes. Digital lesson slides and exercise material are included.

The confirmed rhythms are once or twice a week, which corresponds to 4 or 8 classes per month.

Yes. Portuguese uses the Emotional Speaking approach. Because this is a new offer, this page does not claim past Portuguese results or fixed outcomes.
