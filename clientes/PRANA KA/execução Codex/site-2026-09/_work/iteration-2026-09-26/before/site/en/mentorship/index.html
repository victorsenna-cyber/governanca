I

The crossing has already begun

Perhaps your outer life has already changed.

A relationship ended. The work that once fit no longer does. The city, the routine or the place you held no longer feels right.

Even so, part of you is still waiting for an outside sign before trusting what you already know within.

When that gap stretches on, sensitivity is not what is missing. What is missing is solid ground from which to sustain your own voice.

The point of recognition

You feel deeply. And you have already learned to understand much of what you feel.

Reading, therapy, retreats and practices have opened real paths. The passage before you now asks for integration: bringing what you already know into the boundaries you set, the voice you express and the choices you sustain every day.

“You are not broken. You have simply forgotten yourself.”

Prana Ka

Prana Ka • The Golden Temple

Who guides the crossing

Prana Ka, a doula for life's crossings.

“I guide not because I know everything, but because I live it all.”

A psychoanalyst, psychotherapist, artist, composer and priestess of the womb, Prana brings listening, psychology, ritual and artistic expression together in work that accompanies women through transitions.

Since 2021, the individual version of this journey has been lived and refined. Group mentorship brings that path into a field of shared presence, while preserving each woman's responsibility for her own crossing.

O Templo Dourado is an educational and experiential program. It does not replace psychotherapy, medical care or emergency services.

IV

Dedication is the filter

There comes a moment when recognizing the call also means choosing the path.

This journey speaks to you if

  • you are moving through a transition and your old way of navigating life no longer makes sense
  • you have explored self-knowledge and want to integrate what you know into your body
  • you are willing to bring presence to the practices and the space between sessions
  • you want to recognize boundaries, express your voice and sustain your own choices

It may not yet be the time if

  • you are looking for a solution that does not ask for active participation
  • you want someone else to decide who you should be or how you should live
  • you currently need individual clinical care or emergency support

The group is a space for women willing to make the crossing with presence, respect and responsibility.

V

Questions before the crossing

Clarity is part of care, too.

I'm already in therapy. What does this journey add?

The mentorship brings psychological reflection, body practices, ritual and artistic expression together in a path of integration. It can sit alongside therapy, but does not replace clinical care.

Do I need experience with spiritual practices?

No. You need openness, discernment and a willingness to explore. Every practice will be introduced within the context of the journey, with no previous training required.

How do the 12 sessions work?

There are 12 group sessions combining transmission, practice and integration. The full schedule and frequency will be presented before checkout opens.

How much time do the practices take?

The library includes lessons and practices of around 20 minutes. Your engagement between sessions matters more than performing perfect consistency.

What if I feel afraid to explore something deep?

Fear can be part of a crossing. Here, depth goes hand in hand with respect for boundaries and responsibility. When clinical care is needed, the guidance is to seek appropriate professional support.

When is my place truly confirmed?

When enrollment is open, your place will be confirmed after payment is approved at checkout. The next step will reach you through the channel stated in the offer itself.
