01 /WHAT REMAINS UNSAID

The conversation ends.
But something within you
still wants to speak.

Only later, in the shower, do the words come. The boundary you wanted to set. The desire you swallowed. The “yes” you said when, inside, it was a “no.”

You have built your path, carry responsibilities and have so much to offer. But when it is time to choose yourself, you may end up looking outside for the permission you wish you could feel within.

That is why this session begins with a pause to listen to what is yours.

02 /Visão Uterina

Time to feel.
Space to notice.

In the individual session, Prana guides a meditation and a listening practice focused on the womb and body. The invitation is to notice your desires beyond expectations, fears and the attempt to rationalize everything.

III.

Put words to what you noticed and look at your choices with more presence and trust in yourself.
