What is The Golden Temple?

The Golden Temple

That is the mission, and it explains the rest. The Golden Temple is an initiatory school of feminine wisdom, founded by Prana Ka. It is not a course with modules: it is a stairway, and each step asks more of you than the last.

What is taught here was not invented. It comes from lineages that Prana has walked through, studied and serves.

The lineages

Kashmir Shaivism

Shiva and Shakti, the body as a temple, consciousness that pulses.

Kemet · Ancient Egypt

Aset, Hathor, Heru, Ma'at. Alchemy and the mysteries of the womb.

The Rose · Sumer, Avalon, Magdalene

The priestess lineage. Inanna, the chalice, the red rose.

The four dimensions

Truth is not a thought. It is something you embody.

  1. embodied spirituality
  2. depth psychology
  3. multidimensional
  4. artistic expression

Who the Temple opens for, and who it does not

The Rose calls those who are ready.

Sensitive, creative women, many of them neurodivergent. Those who feel early and deeply. Those who feel a calling and have not yet known how to honor it. Those who want pleasure with soul, not just intensity.

This may not be the time if you are looking for technique without embodiment, ready-made answers or someone to make the crossing for you. Here, no one crosses in your place.

If you want your life to stay the same, don't come near me.

The portal remains open

You did not come here to be desired. You came to bring worlds into being.

From the calling to the coronation, every step is an initiation. The first is choosing where to begin.
