What is The Golden Temple?

Guiding women from exile within themselves to the living temple of their own sovereignty.

That is the mission, and it explains the rest.

The Golden Temple is an initiatory school of feminine wisdom, founded by Prana Ka. It is not a course with modules: it is a stairway, and each step asks more of you than the last.

What is taught here was not invented. It comes from lineages that Prana has walked through, studied and serves.

Your guide

Prana Ka

“I guide not because I know everything, but because I live it all.”

A psychoanalyst, psychotherapist, artist, composer and priestess of the womb, Prana brings listening, psychology, ritual and artistic expression together in work that accompanies women through transitions.

Before any title, she is a woman who has walked through fire and dived deep. She has shed many skins along the way and risen from the ashes as often as she needed to. What she offers comes from that journey, not only from her studies.
