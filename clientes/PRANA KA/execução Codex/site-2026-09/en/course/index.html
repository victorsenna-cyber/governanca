What happened

You are not broken.
You have simply forgotten yourself.

At some point, pleasure became complicated. It grew heavy with guilt, turned into something to get done, or simply disappeared from the map along with your energy for everything else.

05 · Before you decide

“I don't have time.”

Each passage fits into a break in your day. And there is no deadline to finish: the course waits for you, not the other way around.

“I've tried meditation and it didn't work for me.”

Meditation that asks you to empty your mind often does not work for people who feel deeply. These practices do not ask you to stop feeling. They ask the opposite.

“Is this about sex?”

It is about presence. Pleasure here means being alive within your own body, and it shows up in food, music, touch and rest. The rest is a consequence, not the goal.
